# Operations record and runbook

Reuse the governing incident, change or evidence record when present.

- **Action and target** — mode, environment, resource IDs, applicable authority, actor, UTC times and approved window.
- **Intended and observed revision** — source commit, artifact digest or provider version, configuration/schema versions, traffic distribution, and runtime proof.
- **Timeline** — plan/apply/release or incident events, run/operation IDs, actual effects, retries and decisions.
- **Checks** — expected condition, observed result, timestamp/window, sanitized evidence, scope and pass/fail/unverified outcome.
- **Status** — prepared, applied, serving, verified, held, or recovered as supported by evidence. Record blocked checks, owner and next action.

## Repeatable procedure

- Trigger, impact, target and required access.
- Read-only checks and criteria for the next action.
- Scoped mitigation and recovery steps, side effects, stop conditions and escalation.
- Recovery verification, data reconciliation, communication owner and follow-up.
